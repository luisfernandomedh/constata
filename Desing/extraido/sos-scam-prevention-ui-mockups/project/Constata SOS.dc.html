Siete pantallas del microsite responsive, tomando los colores, el tono y las reglas del repo. Sin número de riesgo en pantalla: el veredicto es una frase y la evidencia es la lista de señales, subrayadas dentro del propio mensaje. El caso demo es el robo de código de verificación — el mensaje del banco es auténtico, y por eso mismo es más grave. Las pantallas 1d y 1e son las dos variantes del resultado: estática y conversacional.

No es culpa tuya: ese guion está hecho para funcionar. Lo que sigue tiene orden, y el primero es el que más importa.

  1. 1

    Llama al banco al número del reverso de tu tarjeta

    Diles estas palabras: «me robaron un código de verificación, bloqueen mis accesos». Si hubo transferencia, pregunta por la reversa: según cómo y hace cuánto, todavía puede haberla.

  2. 2

    Guarda las pruebas antes de borrar nada

    Capturas de toda la conversación, el número, y cualquier comprobante. Los chats de estafa se borran solos.

  3. 3

    No le vuelvas a escribir, ni para reclamar

    Reclamar le avisa que sigues ahí, y casi siempre trae una segunda estafa: alguien que ofrece recuperarte el dinero.

  4. 4

    Denuncia con las capturas ya guardadas

    Puede hacerse después de los tres pasos anteriores. Ninguno de ellos espera a la denuncia.

Signals I found

They're asking for the code you just received

The bank sent it so that you could use it. Someone asking you for it means they already have your password, and this is the last step they need.

That number isn't the bank's

It's an ordinary mobile. The bank writes from a short name, and its staff never contact you by chat to ask for anything.

They're rushing you with a charge you didn't make

The amount and the city are there to frighten you. Fear is what makes you act before you think.

Notice

The first message does look like the bank

Which makes it worse, not better. A real code only arrives when someone is signing into your account. If it wasn't you, it was someone else.

What to do now

  1. 1

    Don't reply and don't call that number back. Even if they insist.

  2. 2

    Change your online banking password from the bank's own app, not from any link in the message.

  3. 3

    Call the bank at the number on the back of your card and tell them someone tried to get into your account.

  4. 4

    Save screenshots of the conversation before you block the number.

There's a clock running

That person is inside your account. Call the bank now.

This isn't your fault: that script is built to work. What follows has an order, and the first one matters most.

  1. 1

    Call the bank at the number on the back of your card

    Use these words: "someone stole a verification code from me, block my access". If money moved, ask about a reversal — depending on how it was paid and how long ago, there may still be one.

  2. 2

    Save the evidence before you delete anything

    Screenshots of the whole conversation, the number, and any receipt. Scam chats delete themselves.

  3. 3

    Don't write to them again, not even to complain

    Complaining tells them you're still reachable, and it usually brings a second scam: someone offering to recover your money.

  4. 4

    Report it, using the screenshots you already saved

    This can happen after the three steps above. None of them wait for the report.
